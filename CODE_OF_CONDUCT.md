# Code of Conduct

The Emergent Arcade SDK community should be a place where people can collaborate
without intimidation or exclusion.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when discussing work.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Make space for different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and help move the work forward.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate humiliation,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation against someone who raises a concern
are not welcome. Bad-faith use of project systems, including spam or attempts to expose
security reports, is also unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other spaces where
someone is representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or
lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to
protect the community.

For conduct occurring on GitHub, use GitHub's reporting controls. A concern about a
project maintainer may be directed to another maintainer listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md); anyone involved in a report should recuse themselves
from deciding its outcome. Security vulnerabilities must instead be reported through
the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
